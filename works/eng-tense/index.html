1. Past continuous 過去進行式

was / were + verb-ing

An action was going on at a given time or over a period in the past: We were travelling in Europe last month. David was reading the whole afternoon.
過去某個時間或一段時間內「進行緊」嘅動作。

FormExample
+I/He/She/It was playing; You/We/They were playing
−was not (wasn’t) / were not (weren’t) playing
Yes/No ?Was he running? — Yes, he was. / No, he wasn’t.
Wh- ?What were you doing at 9 p.m.?
Verb-ing form
most verbssing → singing
ends in emake → making
ends in iedie → dying
vowel + consonant (short)run → running; begin → beginning

Tip Usually NOT used with belong, prefer, know, seem.
belong、prefer、know、seem 通常唔用進行式。

2. while and when

two long actions: while … while　·　long + short: when

While Leo was watching TV, Rebecca was reading a book.　(two actions at the same time → past continuous for both, usually while)

While/When we were swimming, a shark suddenly appeared.　(past continuous for the longer action, simple past for the shorter action)

Crystal was quarrelling with her brother when their parents came home.

while / when can go with the longer action; use when for the shorter action.
長動作可以用 while 或 when；短動作只用 when。

3. Past perfect 過去完成式（老師筆記 + SIG 3A）

had + past participle

1. Two past events: the earlier action → past perfect; the later action or the specific time → simple past. The train had already left when Tim arrived at the station. Sandy had written three novels by the time she was eleven.
兩件過去嘅事：先發生用 past perfect，後發生用 past simple。

2. Reported speech: simple past → past perfect; present perfect → past perfect; past perfect stays. “I was a bad student,” said Mrs Au. → Mrs Au said (that) she had been a bad student. “We haven’t finished,” said Tom and Kit. → They said they hadn’t finished.
間接引語：simple past／present perfect 都變 past perfect；past perfect 不變。

3. wish (regret about the past): I wish I’d gone to the cinema. She wishes she hadn’t stayed up late last night.
wish ＋ past perfect：對過去嘅事感到後悔。

Time words just, already, yet, ever, never, when, after, before, by the time.
常見時間詞。

I left when Terence arrived. (I saw him, then I left.)　Sam had left when Terence arrived. (Sam was already gone.)

The man had gone before she opened the door.　Miss Lee emailed us after she had received the parcel.

+I/He/She/They had (’d) left.
−had not (hadn’t) brought her passport
