1. Comparative adjectives 比較級

comparative + than

Compare one person or thing with another: Derek is a better player than Samuel. This book is more interesting than the other one.
用 comparative + than 比較兩個人或事物。

than can be left out if it is clear what we compare: Abby has become a more popular singer recently.
睇得明比較對象時可以省略 than。

Object or subject pronoun after than: Maggie is taller than me. / taller than I (am) (more formal).
than 後面可用賓格或主格代名詞（主格較正式）。

and = change: I am getting heavier and heavier. more and more popular
兩個比較級用 and 連接＝越來越…；長形容詞用 more and more。

the … the … = two things change together: The taller he grew, the slimmer he became.
the + 比較級 … the + 比較級＝越…越…。

2. Superlative adjectives and forms 最高級同變化規則

the + superlative + in / of …

Compare one with a group: Christine is the most intelligent girl in our class.
同一組人或事物比較用 the + superlative。

AdjectiveComparativeSuperlative
most short adj.+ er (taller)+ est (tallest)
short, ends in e+ r (nicer)+ st (nicest)
consonant + yy → ier (heavier)y → iest (heaviest)
vowel + consonantdouble + er (fitter)double + est (fittest)
3+ syllablesmore expensivemost expensive
-ed, -ful, -ing, -lessmore crowded / cheerful / boring / carelessmost …
goodbetterbest
badworseworst
farfarther / furtherfarthest / furthest

Common error No more with -er, no most with -est: ✘ more higher, ✘ the most tallest.
-er 前面唔再加 more；-est 前面唔再加 most。

3. Comparing quantities 比較數量

more / most　·　fewer / fewest　·　less / least

NounComparativeSuperlative
countablemore / fewermost / fewest
uncountablemore / lessmost / least

Sue collected more shells than her brother, but her father collected the most shells.

Sam made fewer mistakes than May, but Chris made the fewest mistakes.

Crystal has less money than Phoebe, but Sally has the least money.

4. as … as, the same as, different from, like, alike 作出比較

as + adj + as　·　not as + adj + as

as … as = the same: I am as tall as my father.　not as … as = not the same: Martin is not as hard-working as Aaron. (= Aaron is more hard-working.)
as … as＝一樣；not as … as＝唔及。

Common error Use the plain adjective in as … as: ✘ as tastier as → ✔ as tasty as.
as … as 中間用原級：唔可以用比較級。

the same (…) as: Jacky is the same age as me.　different from: Maggie is very different from her sister.
the same … as＝一樣；different from＝唔同。

like + noun / pronoun: My dog looks like yours.　alike (no noun after it): Our styles of writing are very much alike.
like 後面跟名詞或代名詞；alike 後面唔跟名詞。
