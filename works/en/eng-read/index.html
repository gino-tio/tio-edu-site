1. Text 2 in short — paragraphs 1-3

Para 1 Derek Shum chats with his wife about a holiday while they prepare breakfast. Soon his social media is bombarded with ads for cruises, tours and package holidays, because their new smart speaker was eavesdropping on them.

Para 2 Because we rely on the Internet, our lives are no longer private: websites track our online behaviour, mobile devices record our movements, smart speakers listen in, and people hack into websites and steal information. We have very little control over who has access to it.

Para 3 Companies track us to deliver targeted ads that match our interests. Targeted ads can be helpful, but the writer feels uncomfortable because the information is used without our knowledge or permission.

2. Text 2 in short — paragraphs 4-5

Para 4 When information falls into the wrong hands the consequences can be severe: people have been threatened, harassed and attacked because of doxing. Pictures are easy to share but can be misleading when taken out of context, so innocent people have become targets. The damage can be long-lasting and extend to family and friends.

Para 5 Protect ourselves: know that our digital footprint can be impossible to remove completely, be careful what we share online, respect other people’s privacy, and always assume that somebody is watching.
