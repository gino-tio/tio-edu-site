3. Answers C–F: Grammar sections

C Tenses: 1 started · 2 has moved · 3 had already bought · 4 had looked · 5 were walking · 6 were taking · 7 were riding · 8 had explored · 9 will visit · 10 enjoy.

C2: “moved out recently” still matters now → present perfect. C4: Dad finished looking before another past action → past perfect.

D Conditionals: 1 violate · 2 have · 3 are not allowed · 4 need · 5 will find · 6 is · 7 are not · 8 should avoid · 9 follow · 10 will enjoy.

D1, D3: these are rules that are always true → zero conditional (If + present, present), no “will”.

4. G Proofreading

G (“The Care Bears”): (e.g.) on → in · (e.g.) ^was · 1 ^were · 2 ^had · 3 after → before · 4 plan → planned · 5 airring → airing · 6 played → playing · 7 ^had · 8 happiest → happy / happier · 9 grew → grown · 10 face → faces.

Tested: past continuous (were working, was airing), past perfect (had received, had planned, had become), present perfect (has grown), subject–verb agreement (any bear faces), spelling (airing).
