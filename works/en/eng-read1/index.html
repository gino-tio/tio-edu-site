1. Text 1 — How big data improves online shopping (p.4-5)

Para 1 Amazon began by selling books online; today online shopping is commonplace. The writer thinks it gives a far more customized experience than bricks-and-mortar shops.

Para 2 Every click is tracked; this data is combined with information from social media and search engine queries to create big data. Companies analyse it to find patterns and trends, formulate strategies and drive sales.

Para 3 Marketing can match customers’ preferences: targeted emails instead of generic ones (boutique, online pharmacy, cosmetics shop, florist). The writer believes individualized marketing beats blanket advertising.

Para 4 Businesses optimize site design from data; asking customers directly was lengthy and cumbersome. They can be more responsive and give bestselling products a more prominent position.

Para 5 Customer service: the writer’s problem at an online optician’s was solved by a message with a link to his basket, plus a free-shipping coupon — a favourable impression.

Para 6 The writer would rather visit online shops that tailor the experience to his specific needs than shops that treat everyone the same.

2. Text 2 — Support local artisans at Handcrafted! (p.8-9)

A leaflet for a monthly market (8 a.m.–8 p.m., first Sunday of every month, West Kowloon Cultural District) selling locally designed, handmade products.

Reasons to visit sustainable shopping (local sources, recycled materials); bargain hunters find great deals on unique items; vendors are happy to haggle; special offer: a free reusable shopping bag if you spend $200 or more.

What you’ll find 132 stalls (snacks, gifts, homeware, stylish furniture by young local artisans), a few second-hand dealers selling rare vintage items, workshops (try your hand at making accessories), game stalls and live music from local indie bands.

Getting there / contact MTR to Kowloon Station (Tung Chung Line) or Green Minibus 77M to Austin Road West; website and phone for enquiries.
