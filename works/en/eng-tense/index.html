1. Past continuous

was / were + verb-ing

An action in progress at a time in the past: At 8 p.m. last night, I was doing my homework.

FormExample
+I was doing / They were playing
−I wasn’t doing / They weren’t playing
?Was he sleeping? / What were you doing?

Verbs such as know, like, want are not usually used in the continuous.

2. when and while

while + past continuous　·　when + past simple

I was walking home when it started to rain.　(long action interrupted by a short one)

While Dad was cooking, Mum was reading a book.　(two actions at the same time)

3. Past perfect

had + past participle

The earlier of two past actions: When I arrived, the film had already started.

Time words: already, just, never … before, by the time, before, after.

FormExample
+She had finished.
−She hadn’t finished.
?Had you seen it before?

4. Choosing the tense

Past simple = a finished action: I watched TV.

Past continuous = in progress at that time: I was watching TV at 8.

Past perfect = happened before another past time: I had watched TV before Dad came home.
