1. Scale

Representative fraction 1 : 5,000 → 1 cm to 50 m

Conversion: in 1 : n, 1 cm stands for n cm. Divide n by 100 to get “1 cm to ? m”.
E.g. 1 : 20,000 → 1 cm to 200 m; 1 : 5,000 → 1 cm to 50 m.

Statement scale: 1 cm to 50 m　Linear scale: draw a ruler marked 0, 50, 100 … 250 m.

Real distance = map length × scale.

QE example: East Rail Line 6.1 cm on the map × 200 m = 1,220 m (1,200–1,240 m accepted).

2. Grid references

Easting first, then northing

4-figure: refers to a grid square (easting + northing of its lower-left corner), e.g. 1865.
6-figure: divide each square into tenths; 3-digit easting + 3-digit northing, e.g. 182656.

UT example: Legislative Council Complex, 4-figure = 1667; church along Hennessy Road, 6-figure = 182656.

3. Direction

Compass points / whole-circle bearing / reduced bearing

Compass points (8 directions): N 0°, NE 45°, E 90°, SE 135°, S 180°, SW 225°, W 270°, NW 315°.

Whole-circle bearing: measured clockwise from north, 0°–360°, written with three digits (e.g. 027°). ±2° is accepted.

Reduced bearing: measured from north (N) or south (S) towards east (E) or west (W), up to 90°, e.g. N 45° W.

Conversion: NE: WCB = θ; SE: WCB = 180° − θ; SW: WCB = 180° + θ; NW: WCB = 360° − θ.

QE example: police station (073721) from fire station → SW, 222°; restricted access from spot height 305 → ENE, 84°.

UT example: shopping mall (177672) from spot height 53.1 (162644) → 027°; cinema from primary school → N 45° W.

Tip: “direction of B from A” = stand at A and look at B.

4. Conventional signs

Common signs

Declared monument, Government office, Trigonometric station, Spot height, Church, Hotel, Playground, Kindergarten, Secondary school, Petrol station, Car park, Post office, Clinic, Fire station, Police station.

If you do not know a sign, check the legend of the map extract and practise often.
